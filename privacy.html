1. Overview & Local-First Architecture

Adung Studio Book (developed by Adung Studio, website: https://claudeadung.space/) is an independent desktop software application built for Windows. We are committed to protecting your privacy and upholding data minimization principles.

By architectural design, Adung Studio Book is a local-first application. Your project databases (SQLite), scripts, audio voice clips, storyboard frames, and rendered MP4 files reside exclusively on your local workstation. We do not operate intermediary cloud relay servers to store or inspect your creative manuscripts.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect or store your creative manuscripts, audio narration, or video renders.
  • We do not record keystrokes, clipboard data, or private user directories.
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize personal user data under any circumstances.

3. Third-Party AI Services & BYOK Privacy

Adung Studio Book offers opt-in direct integration with Anthropic Claude Messages API. When you configure your own API key (ADUNG_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY):

  • API requests are transmitted directly and securely from your local machine to Anthropic's official endpoint (https://api.anthropic.com) via TLS encryption.
  • Your API keys are stored locally on your machine and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • All API requests are subject to Anthropic's Commercial Privacy Policy and Data Retention standards.

4. Website Analytics & Cookies

Our distribution website (claudeadung.space) does not deploy intrusive tracking cookies, third-party pixel trackers, or cross-site behavioral advertising tools. Minimal technical server logs (such as IP addresses and HTTP user-agents) may be recorded by hosting infrastructure solely for security and DDoS mitigation.
